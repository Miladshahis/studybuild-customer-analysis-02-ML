# StudyBuild | Project 02
## Customer Analytics & A/B Testing

**Customer Behavior | Campaign Effectiveness | Data-Driven Decision Making**

This project is part of the **StudyBuild Project-Based Mentorship Program**.

Participants work on a real-world customer analytics challenge, exploring customer behavior, marketing campaign performance, and A/B testing to support data-driven business decisions.

### Key Focus Areas
- Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis
- Customer Behavior & Segmentation
- Marketing Campaign Analysis
- A/B Testing & Statistical Inference
- Business Insights & Recommendations

### Project Submission
Participants complete their projects independently, submit their work through GitHub, and receive mentor feedback.

**Full project instructions and requirements are shared with StudyBuild participants.**
